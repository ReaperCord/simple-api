  GET /openapi/v2 (application/json)                                     -> 200, 131 ocorrencias de "ArbitraryKeys",

ConfigMap) e o cache de cluster do Argo CD v3.5.5. Referencia: https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/metrics-server/issues/1868
